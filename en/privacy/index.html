Audidict Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026.

Audidict is an app that turns what you say into a clean note. This policy explains which data is used for that, by whom, why and for how long. In short: your notes and recordings stay on your own phone. A recording only goes to two AI services to be processed, and they don't use your data to train their AI. You don't need an account.

Who is responsible

Audidict is made by Steffen Maayen (Maayen Studio), Parvatistraat 21, 1363 XG Almere, the Netherlands. Send privacy questions to maayen.studio@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

This data is stored only on your phone and goes nowhere else:

  • your recordings (the audio files);
  • your notes, the transcripts and your own edits;
  • your own formats and your word list;
  • your settings.

It is included in your phone's regular iCloud backup if you have that turned on. If you delete a note or the app, it is gone from your phone.

What happens when a recording is processed

A recording is only processed after you have given consent in the app. You can withdraw that consent at any time in Settings, under AI consent. After that, nothing is sent.

  1. Your recording goes to ElevenLabs (United States), which turns it into text. Your word list is sent along, so names and terms are recognized correctly. As soon as the text is back, we have ElevenLabs delete the recording and the text. ElevenLabs does keep backups for up to 30 days. ElevenLabs does not use your data to train its AI.
  2. The text goes to Anthropic (United States), which turns it into a clean note or fills in your format. Anthropic does not use your data to train its AI and deletes it within 30 days. Only text flagged by Anthropic's safety checks may be kept for up to 2 years.
  3. Our server (Supabase, in Frankfurt) passes the recording and the text along. The server does not keep your recording or text.

Processing happens over an encrypted connection.

What our server does keep

To limit the free version fairly and to prevent abuse at your expense and ours, our server keeps the following per installation:

  • a random number for your installation, which says nothing about you (created by RevenueCat, see below);
  • per recording: a random recording number, the month, the length and whether it was a note or an addition;
  • whether you have a subscription;
  • a security key from Apple (App Attest) that proves a request comes from the real Audidict app on a real iPhone. This is a public key; it says nothing about you.

No name, no email address, no content of your recordings or notes. We keep this data for 13 months; after that it is deleted automatically.

With every request the server technically also sees your IP address. It is not stored with your data; Supabase keeps it briefly in its logs for security and troubleshooting.

Subscription and payment

Payment goes through Apple. We don't see your payment details. RevenueCat (United States) keeps track of whether you have a subscription for us, so you can restore it on a new phone. For that, RevenueCat receives the random installation number and Apple's purchase details, but not your name or email address.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, it sends a technical crash report to Sentry, which stores it in the European Union: where in the app it went wrong, your phone model and the version of iOS and the app. Recordings, notes, transcripts or screenshots are never included. This lets us fix bugs.

What we don't do

  • No account, no email address, no name.
  • No ads and no tracking. We don't follow you across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell data.
  • Your data is not used to train AI.

Data outside the European Union

ElevenLabs, Anthropic and RevenueCat are American companies and process data in the United States. Sentry stores crash reports in the EU but is also an American company. These transfers are protected by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. We have a data processing agreement with each provider.

Legal basis

  • Processing your recordings: because you use the app to make notes (performance of the contract), and with your explicit consent to the use of AI.
  • Usage limits, subscription and security: to provide the app and prevent abuse (performance of the contract and legitimate interest).
  • Crash reports: to keep the app working properly (legitimate interest).

Your rights

You can ask us which data we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted. Because we don't know your name or email address, we can only find data if you give us your installation ID. You'll find it in the app at the bottom of Settings. You can also object, and file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl) or the authority in your own country.

You can delete most data yourself: delete a note or the app, and withdraw your AI consent.

Children

Audidict is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

If we change this policy, you'll see it in the date at the top. For major changes, we'll let you know in the app.
